📈 Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard – Excel Project

Welcome to my **Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard** project. This project showcases an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, gross profit, profitability, and country-wise business performance using data visualization and interactive slicers.

🚀 About This Project

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and interactive slicers. It helps users monitor business performance, compare revenue and profit trends, and identify key markets.

📊 Dashboard Highlights

- 💰 **Total Revenue:** 645,223.18
- 📈 **Total Gross Profit:** 296,384.25
- 🎯 **Gross Profit Margin:** 45.94%
- 📦 **Total Units Sold:** 6,698
- 🛒 **Total Orders:** 2,707
- 💵 **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** 348,838.93

📉 Key Visualizations

- **Monthly Revenue & Profit:** Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- **Top Countries by Revenue:** Compares country-wise revenue performance.
- **Revenue Share by Channel:** Analyzes revenue contribution across sales channels.
- **Region-wise Comparison:** Compares sales performance across European regions.
- **Country-wise Map Chart:** Visualizes geographical revenue distribution.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter results by product category, country, and quarter.
